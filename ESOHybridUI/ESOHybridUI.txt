## Title: ESO Hybrid UI
## Description: Controller-first ESO interface. Keep Gamepad Mode On while Hybrid uses keyboard-layout menus with a controller-operated pointer and a compact keyboard HUD. Protected/native workflows such as loot and lockpicking retain ESO's native handling. Requires LibAddonMenu-2.0 r43+. Optional: LibMousePointer (controller cursor), LibGamepadOptions (controller settings page), LibDebugLogger (persistent log output). Inspired by Advanced Disable Controller UI (Lionas; contributions: jc28735250 and DoriKyll).
